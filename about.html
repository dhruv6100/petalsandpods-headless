How it began

The journey from gift to ritual

2022

A gift that changed everything

A handmade bar soap received during the holidays sparked a question — why don't more people have access to this? The Nalupu / Ubtan recipe from India, 5,000 years old, became the foundation.

2023

First batches, pressed by hand

Small-batch production began in San Francisco. Every stick pressed by hand, sourced from traceable ingredients, packaged with zero-waste intent. The Glass Skin Miracle Cleanser was born.

2024

Jazba Collective · Farmers Markets · Craft Fairs

Petals & Pods found its community — at farmers markets, handmade craft fairs, and through the Jazba Collective Accelerator. Real customers, real stories, real skin transformations.

2025

Two sticks. Two rituals. One belief.

The Brightening One-Step Glow Cleanser joined the collection. Expanding the range while refusing to scale the soul of what makes each stick special — hand-pressed, small batch, always.

Now

Exploring more ancient recipes

We are yet to explore more recipes. A formula creator intern from Milpitas is joining to research and revive time-proven skincare traditions from diverse global cultures. The journey has just begun.

What drives us

Our principles don't change
with the seasons.

01

Earth first, always

Anti-consumerism isn't a marketing line for us. One stick, two uses, morning and evening. Less is genuinely more.

We are living proof that commercial success and ethical practice are not at odds — they are the same path, walked with intention.
