Founder's Story

Business and conscious living are two sides of the same coin.

A warm, loving gift received during the 2022 holidays — in the form of handmade bar soap — transformed into today's Petals & Pods face wash sticks.

I have always been someone who has been a sincere explorer in the space of self-knowing. This business has become one of the ways my life expresses itself. This is also my quest to live and prove that business and conscious living are not extreme ends — but two sides of the same coin.

Founded on a 5,000-year-old skincare recipe, Nalupu / Ubtan from India — we are yet to explore more recipes.

Our process

Food for skin.

Three rituals, one philosophy. We don't manufacture skincare — we cook it, slowly, with intention.

01
Ingredients

Sourced like food, not chemicals.

Whole roots, hand-picked petals, sun-dried herbs — we work with growers who know the soil their plants came from. Nothing synthetic. Nothing rushed.

02
Recipes

Recipes from kitchens, not labs.

Our formulations come from Ayurvedic recipes our grandmothers used at home. We ferment our roses and rice for 60 days. We grind by hand.

03
Packaging

Packaging that returns to the earth.

Compostable tubes. Plant-based wrappers. Glass that's recyclable and refillable. Designed to dissolve back into the same world it came from.
