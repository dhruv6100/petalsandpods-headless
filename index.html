Founder's Story

Business and conscious living are two sides of the same coin.

A warm, loving gift received during the 2022 holidays — in the form of handmade bar soap — transformed into today's Petals & Pods face wash sticks.

I have always been someone who has been a sincere explorer in the space of self-knowing. This business has become one of the ways my life expresses itself. This is also my quest to live and prove that business and conscious living are not extreme ends — but two sides of the same coin.

Founded on a 5,000-year-old skincare recipe, Nalupu / Ubtan from India — we are yet to explore more recipes.
